
\begin{recipe}{7-up Party Salad}\tag{jello}
  \rating    5
  \nutrition 1
  \health    1
  \workload{heavy}
  \time{120}
  \yield{1 jello mold}
  \servings{10}
  \source{Bonnie Schermer}
  % \photo{}
  \maketitle

  \begin{ingredients2}
    7 \oz & 7-up\\
    16 & large marshmallows\\
    3 \oz & lime jello\\
    6 \oz & cream cheese\\
    20 \oz & crushed pineapple\\
    \twothird \cup & Light Miracle Whip\\
    1 \cup & heavy cream\\
    1 \T & powdered sugar
  \end{ingredients2}

  Pour off the juice of the crushed pineapple, but do not press all liquid out.

  Combine and melt in a double boiler (or 2 cup measuring cup and pan) the 7-up and
  marshmallows. Make sure the mixture is hot and add jello. Stir until dissolved. In
  a blender, mix cream cheese and crushed pineapple. Pour into medium mixing bowl,
  add the jello mixture, and whisk.  Chill 45 minutes. 

  Whip cream with sugar and add Miracle Whip after the cream fluffs up. Add blender
  contents to cream and mix together gently with a spoon. Pour into lightly oiled
  mold and put in freezer to set.
\end{recipe}
