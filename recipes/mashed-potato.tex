
\begin{recipe}{Mashed Potatoes}\tag{potato}
  \rating    5
  \nutrition 3
  \health    3
  \workload{light}
  \time{30}
%   \yield{}
%   \servings{}
%   \source{}
  % \photo{}
  \maketitle

  % Tests:
  % - 3 potatoes: 1/4 t salt, 4 T butter: could use a bit more salt, butter was ok.

  Crush some cloves of garlic and soak in a small amount of oil or butter. For an
  upgrade: Simmer garlic in buttermilk and a splash of whipping cream on low about 15
  minutes --- do not boil!

  Soften or melt about 1--2~\T butter per potato.

  Use mixture of russets and red potatoes. Peel about half of the potatoes. Put in
  pot and fill with hot tap water just to top of potatoes. Add salt (\eighth~\t per
  potato) cover and bring to boil on high, but do not allow to come to full rolling
  boil. Potatoes should be ready after a few minutes at low boil.

  Drain potatoes, add pepper and check saltiness. Mash and add garlic and dairy in a
  few batches. Add some small splashes of milk if the potatoes are dry (typically
  needed). Don't overdo mashing.
\end{recipe}
