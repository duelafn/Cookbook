
\begin{recipe}{No-Bake Cookies}%\tag{}
  \rating    3
  \nutrition 0
  \health    0
  \workload{medium}
  \time{60}
  \yield{30}
  \servings{}
  \source{Dean}
  % \photo{}
  \maketitle

  \begin{ingredients2}
    \half \c & butter\\% 114 g
    \half \c & milk\\% 114 g
    \threefourth \c & brown sugar\\% 180 g
    \threefourth \c & sugar\\% 160 g
    2 \oz & bakers chocolate\\% 57 g
    \twothird \c & peanut butter\\% 175 g  (85 g fat, 16 g sugar)
    3 \c & quick oats\\% 250 g
    1 \T & vanilla
  \end{ingredients2}% -> 1050 g

  Bring butter, milk, sugars, and chocolate to full boil over medium-low heat. Stir
  in oats, then peanut butter and vanilla. Let cool a bit and drop on waxed paper.

  Note: JIF PB makes too soft (have to refrigerate), try ground peanuts.
