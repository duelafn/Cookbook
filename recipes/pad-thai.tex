  \begin{ingredients2}
    1 \lb & rice noodles\\
    1 \lb & chicken thighs\\
    \quarter \c & chicken stock\\
    \quarter \c & ketchup\\
    2 \T & sugar\\
    2 \T & sweet thai chili\\
    1 & lime zest\\
    2 clove & garlic\\
    2 & eggs\\
      & bean sprouts\\
      & scallions\\
    \quarter \c & crushed roasted peanuts
  \end{ingredients2}

  Soak noodles in hot water 30~min then drain.

  Mix sauce (chicken stock, ketchup, sugar, chili sauce, lime).

  Heat wok with 2~\T peanut oil over medium heat. Cook chicken until just done, push
  aside. Cook garlic briefly, add eggs and scramble lightly, merge in the chicken.
  Add noodles and sauce, toss continuously, add splahes of water if it starts to dry
  out. Cook until noodles done (2--5 min). Add sprouts, scallions, and peanuts. Toss
  and serve.
